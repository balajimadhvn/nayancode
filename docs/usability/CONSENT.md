# Participant information and consent form

**Study:** Usability of the Nayancode Workbench, a free web tool for biological data analysis
**Researcher:** Balaji Madhavan, Vivofold (support@vivofold.in)
**Ethics reference:** ____________

## What is this study?
We are testing how easy it is to use the Nayancode Workbench. You will try five short analysis tasks in a web
browser while saying aloud what you are thinking, then fill in a 10-question form. We are testing the software,
not you: if something is hard, that is a problem with the software.

## What will happen?
The session takes about 45 minutes. A researcher will watch your screen and take notes. Nothing is installed on
your computer, and the data used are public research data.

## Do I have to take part?
No. Taking part is voluntary. **If you are a Vedicgen student, your decision and your answers will not affect your
grades, certificate, fees or how you are treated.** You can stop at any time without giving a reason, and you can
ask for your data to be removed until the analysis is complete (planned date: ____________).

## What data are kept?
Your answers are stored under a code (for example P07), not your name. We keep: how long each task took, whether it
was completed, the number of hints, notes on where you got stuck, your questionnaire answers, and your comments.
This form, which has your name, is stored separately from the data and is destroyed ____ years after the study.
Results are published only as group summaries and anonymous quotes.

## Risks and benefits
There are no expected risks beyond mild frustration with the tasks. There is no payment. Your feedback will
improve a free tool for students and researchers.

## Questions or complaints
Contact support@vivofold.in, or the ethics committee named above.
